# Milestone 1 design

The screen is a recruiter’s evidence worksheet: confirm the role on the left, inspect one candidate’s evidence on the right. The three steps reflect the approved flow, rather than adding a dashboard.

- Typeface: locally bundled Source Sans 3 for readable documents and controls.
- Colors: ink #172c42, slate #526579, canvas #edf2f7, paper #ffffff, blue #245c99, green #216344.
- Left-aligned text, generous separation between stages, compact requirement rows.
- Quotes sit beside the requirement they support, with an option to inspect the complete extracted text.
- Status always uses words as well as color. Keyboard focus is visible; the layout becomes one column on phones.
- No marketing claims, illustrative candidates, ranking, or saving controls.
