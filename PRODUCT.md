1 · The job
When applications pile up for an open role, I want to identify the strongest candidates without reading every CV, so I can give the hiring manager a shortlist I can defend. Today the recruiter scans CVs manually, uses ATS filters, and compares profiles against the JD.

2 · The switch
Anxiety: it might misunderstand the role or miss a strong candidate. Habit: reading CVs myself feels safer. So the product confirms what the role requires, ranks candidates with evidence, flags uncertainty, and lets the recruiter override it.

3 · The core flow
Today: applications arrive → read CVs → compare against JD → shortlist → send to hiring manager. With my product: add JD → confirm requirements → upload CVs → ranked candidates with evidence → review or override → shortlist → export. Must not: rank against unconfirmed requirements, hide low-ranked or unreadable CVs, invent evidence, or recommend someone without showing why.

4 · Onboarding
The recruiter gives one JD and a few CVs, nothing more. Add JD → confirm requirements → upload CVs → first ranked candidates with evidence. No ATS integration or company setup. The worry it removes: “it might miss someone I would have picked.”

5 · v1
Does: JD and CV upload, confirm requirements, evidence-backed ranking, flag unreadable or uncertain candidates, recruiter override, shortlist, evidence-backed export, save and reopen. Doesn't: sourcing, JD writing, publishing, interviews, fraud detection, scheduling, ATS integrations, offers or onboarding. Success: the recruiter returns with another role or batch of CVs.

6 · Riskiest guess
The product can shortlist strong candidates without making the recruiter redo the work. Test 10 CVs against a recruiter’s independent shortlist: every recruiter pick must appear in the product’s recommended shortlist before overrides, and every claim must be supported by the CV. Then compare manual vs product-assisted shortlisting on separate unseen batches. Pass if the product is faster, requires ≤2 CV reopenings and ≤2 material corrections. Actual result: not tested yet.

7 · Milestones
I can upload a JD, confirm its requirements and see evidence for or against one candidate; I can flag insufficient evidence; I can rank ten readable CVs while keeping unreadable ones visible as unable to assess; I can explain the ranking; I can override it; I can export an evidence-backed shortlist; I can close it, reopen it, and my data is still there.
